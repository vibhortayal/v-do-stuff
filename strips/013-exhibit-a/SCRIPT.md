## Slide 1 — The Scene of the Crime
Kitchen, late afternoon — wall clock reads 4:47 PM. Wife walks out of the work room,
laptop under her arm, and stops in the doorway. The sink is full of dishes. On the
counter: Vibhor's lunch plate, untouched since noon, next to an empty coffee mug.
Vibhor sits at the counter mid-scroll on his phone, biscuit in the other hand,
completely unbothered. Nobody speaks. She looks at him. Then at the plate.
Then back at him.
- Dialogue: none.
- Caption: "No one had spoken yet. Everything had been said."

## Slide 2 — The Charge Sheet
Same kitchen, moments later. Wife reads the charges, counting them off on her
fingers. Vibhor, defensive, phone still in hand.
- Wife: "Lunch: untouched. Dishes: untouched. You: on that phone since morning."
- Vibhor: "It's called research."
- Wife: "On Instagram?"
- Caption: "The prosecution rests."

## Slide 3 — The Verdict
Evening, same kitchen, sink now empty. Both at the sink together — Vibhor washing,
Wife drying, easy rhythm, fight already forgotten. His phone is propped on the
windowsill playing a reel, and both of them are watching it while they work.
- Wife: "Same time tomorrow?"
- Vibhor: "Only if you eat lunch."
- Wife: "That was YOUR lunch."
- Caption: "Verdict: guilty. Sentence: dishes, together."
