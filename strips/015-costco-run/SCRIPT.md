# Strip 015 - "Just the Essentials"

Series: v_do_stuff - 3-slide carousel - flat comic style (thick ink outlines, 2-tone cel shading), characters per LOCK v1.3
Status: Draft script by Spark (2026-10-26). Awaiting Vibhor's approval - no art yet.
Theme: slice of life - a three-item list meets a warehouse. Nobody survives Costco.

## Slide 1 - The List
Kitchen, morning (white cabinets, light stone counters - house canon). Wife hands Vibhor a sticky note. On it, huge and lonely: MILK / EGGS / SPINACH. She is calm. He has his keys and total confidence.
- Wife: "Costco. Three things. Milk, eggs, spinach."
- Vibhor: "In and out. Twenty minutes."
- Caption: "The list had three things on it."

## Slide 2 - The Warehouse
Costco aisle, pallets stacked to the ceiling. Vibhor pushes an overflowing industrial cart: a kayak balanced on top, a giant teddy bear riding in the child seat, a 5-gallon tub of mayo, 48 muffins, a tower of toilet paper. Sample cup in his free hand, bliss on his face.
- Vibhor (to himself): "A kayak is basically savings."
- Caption: "It was not twenty minutes."

## Slide 3 - The Reveal
The apartment, buried. Bulk boxes stacked to the ceiling, the teddy bear seated at the table like a tenant, toilet-paper tower in the corner. In the background, the hall closet door bulges - still full from the guests (strip 014). Vibhor half-hidden behind the paper towels, paddle in hand. Wife in the doorway, sticky note held up like evidence.
- Wife: "Where's the milk?"
- Vibhor: "...I got a kayak."
- Caption (her line): "You are never going alone again."
